How Fusion decides

The lightest pattern that does the job. Extra model calls, in blue, only when needed.3

Standard previously Auto

One model; balances cost, quality and speed. All plans.

High previously HydraFusion

Extra calls on substantial tasks; saves some tokens. Pro+, Max, Business, Enterprise.

Max previously HydraFusion Max

Deepest reasoning, token-efficient. Same plans.
